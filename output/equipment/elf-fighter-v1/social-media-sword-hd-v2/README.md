# Sword HD social images

Two original 2160 × 2700 PNG renders (4:5), front and 25.7-degree angled views. Rendered locally with Three.js r180 from the original Tripo GLB and its embedded 2048² texture. No AI redraw, mesh edit, texture regeneration or Tripo credits used. Tripo production total remains 110 credits.

Use these as clean final-result images. They are local renders of the Tripo output, not screenshots of Tripo Studio. The original Studio screenshots remain in social-media-sword-v1 as workflow evidence.

The larger image improves framing and edge resolution; it does not create texture detail absent from the original 2K atlas. Original material settings retained, with neutral presentation lighting and an off-white background. Renderer source: ../sword-hd-render.html (requires HTTP serving and access to pinned Three.js CDN modules).
